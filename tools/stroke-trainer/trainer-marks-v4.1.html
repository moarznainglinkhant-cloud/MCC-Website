1 · Bands. Every stroke of the letter is a band: two borders from the font running side by side between two ends. The trainer finds the ends (the flat cuts, marked with small dots). If an end is in the wrong place, drag its dots along the border. A shape that isn't a simple band shows in red (for example a bowl closed by a crossbar): drag a cut line across it where the pen would lift, and it splits into bands. Drag a cut's end dots to move them; click a cut and press Delete to remove it.
2 · Order & direction. Click each band, in writing order, near the end where the pen starts. Click near its other end to turn it round; click near its start again to take it out. A ring starts on the vertical line through its centre (dashed), at the top or the bottom, and the start is cut exactly along that line. Click the quarter the pen heads into: top-right starts at the top going right (clockwise), top-left at the top going left, and the same for the bottom. Click the same quarter again to take it out.
